Artists & collaboration

Your creative bathroom

  • Tools: Choose paste colors at the vanity. Blend them in the soap dish. Pick up the toothbrush, mouthwash spray, sponge or duck stamp at their natural locations.
  • Pattern craft: Pick up the rubber stamp beside the shower, then fit a duck, bubbles, flower or tile insert from the utility-room wall above the camera. R / Turn rotates the design while holding the stamp. Colors and nozzle sizes work with every insert. Put the stamp down with Q before turning a paper roll.
  • Save artwork: Use the SAVE ART tray beside the utility-room camera to download the last painted towel or roll at its native canvas resolution, without a frame. Wait for all marks to save first. After a reload, the tray defaults to the current towel until you paint another surface.
  • Spare rolls: Open the vanity doors. Carry a roll with E, aim at a display stand or clear floor and use E to place it. Put it down before painting. Q returns it to its previous spot. Aim at a roll and press R / Turn to rotate it a quarter turn. Each wrapping surface saves its own artwork.
  • Utility drawers: Step back, then open the three drawers below the camera. The top holds a VHS cassette that toggles tape playback, the middle holds pastel pigment, and the bottom holds a deck of creative postcards.
  • Cabinet storage: Carry the jar, washcloth or spare rolls between glowing empty storage markers. Step back before swinging a door.
  • Towels: Choose a size from the shelf. Other towels keep their drawings. The owner can adjust the wall rail. Carry the stool by its side, place it on clear floor, then use its top to climb.
  • Laundry: Use the washer door to load the hanging towel, then its start button for 20 seconds. Collect the wet bundle from the washer door, load it through the dryer door, then use the dryer start button for 20 seconds. It returns clean to the rail. Roll artwork stays separate.
  • Your label: Paint on a towel or roll, then use FRAME LABEL beside the instant camera. Drag the crop, adjust zoom and placement, and apply the preview to your body. A saved label survives washing its source.
  • Instant gallery: Paint on a towel or spare roll, then use the instant camera on the utility-room cabinet. It alternates between two frames on the bathroom dividing wall. A print preserves that moment, even after washing. Use a framed print to download its PNG.
  • Steam: Run the hot shower, pull back the curtain and draw through the mirror fog. The extractor clears the condensation. Mirror drawings fade in 45 seconds.

Performance & display

Laundry motion is enabled independently of device reduced-motion settings. Turn it off here for still drums.

Tape adds correlated fine grain, color noise, head-switch shimmer, light tracking streaks and a recording clock and occasional fading static. Subtle mode respects reduced motion. Menus and controls stay clear.

Touch: move with the left controls, drag LOOK or the room to look around, hold Paint and tap Use, Turn or Put down.

Touch devices start with Mobile; computers start with Fast. Your choice is remembered. The interface stays sharp; only the 3D view uses fewer pixels.
